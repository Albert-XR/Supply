## Information You Send Us

When you submit an enquiry through our contact form, we receive the details you provide — such as your name, email address, company, country and product requirements. We use this information only to reply to your enquiry and to prepare a quotation. We do not sell or rent it. Enquiries are recorded in our customer relationship management system so that our sales team can follow them up.

## Analytics and Cookies

This website uses **Google Analytics 4** and **Microsoft Clarity** to understand how visitors use the site — for example, which pages are visited and how visitors move between them. These services may set cookies and process aggregated usage data, including an approximate location derived from your IP address.

We also use **OKKI CRM**, a customer relationship management service, to record enquiries and to understand which pages visitors view before getting in touch. Its tracking code collects aggregated page-view data, and the details you submit through an enquiry form may be stored in OKKI CRM so that we can respond.

It also records which buttons and links visitors use (for example WhatsApp or "get a quote" clicks) so that we can improve the site. This data is aggregated and is not used to identify you personally.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time — the site will continue to work normally.

## Third-party Services

Data may be processed by the providers of the services above (Google, Microsoft and Xiaoman Technology, the operator of OKKI CRM) and by our form-handling provider, solely to operate this website and respond to enquiries. Links on this site to other websites (for example social media) are governed by those websites' own privacy policies.

## Your Choices

You may ask us to delete the details you have sent us, or to stop using them, at any time by emailing [sales@ding-yong.com](mailto:sales@ding-yong.com).
