## Shipping routes at a glance

| Item | Options |
|---|---|
| Loading ports | Shenzhen / Guangzhou / Shantou |
| Mode | Sea freight (FCL or LCL), air freight, express (DHL / FedEx / UPS) |
| Trade terms | FOB Shenzhen / Guangzhou, CIF, DDP for selected destinations |
| Insurance | All-risk marine insurance available |
| West Africa gateways | Tema (Ghana), Lome (Togo); Dakar or Abidjan for inland West Africa |

For Ghana and Togo, cargo discharges at a coastal port and clears locally. For **landlocked Mali**, the container is discharged at **Dakar (Senegal)** or **Abidjan (Cote d'Ivoire)** and moves on by road or rail — this is why the destination country matters as much as the port when the packing plan is drawn up.

## What a China-to-Africa supply case actually involves

A cutlery supply case is a chain of handovers, not a single shipment. The stages below are the ones buyers most often ask us to confirm before committing:

| Stage | What is confirmed | Who provides it |
|---|---|---|
| Product specification | Set composition, steel grade, finish, handle construction, packaging | Buyer and factory together |
| Sample approval | An approved counter sample kept as the production reference | Factory |
| Production capacity | Monthly output, headcount and production lines matched to the order | Factory |
| Pre-shipment inspection | Inspection record before loading | Factory or appointed third party |
| Packing plan | Carton size, sets per carton, gross weight | Confirmed per order against destination |
| Export documents | Commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin where required | Factory and forwarder |
| Inland transit | Dakar or Abidjan transhipment for landlocked destinations | Forwarder |

DINGYONG is the manufacturer at the first stage of this chain: a **5,000 m² plant in Jiedong District, Jieyang**, with **200+ workers**, **10 million+ pieces of monthly output** and **12+ years** of cutlery production. Specification changes, tooling, polishing and packing are handled in-house rather than brokered to another producer — see [how to tell a cutlery factory from a trading company](/answers/how-to-tell-a-cutlery-factory-from-a-trading-company/) for the checks behind that claim.

Carton dimensions and sets per carton are not published as fixed figures: they are confirmed per order against your set configuration and destination. Ask for the packing data for your specific reference when you [request a quote](/contact/).

## What to settle before the goods are booked

1. **Specification** — set composition, steel grade, thickness or weight, finish, solid or hollow handle, and packaging. Two sets can look identical in a photo and be completely different products in the hand.
2. **Packing plan** — carton size, sets per carton and gross weight are confirmed per order against your quantity and destination.
3. **Samples** — samples are available on request and refundable on bulk orders.
4. **Inspection** — every order goes through sample confirmation and pre-shipment inspection; third-party testing can be arranged on request.
