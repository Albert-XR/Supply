## Shipping routes at a glance

| Item | Options |
|---|---|
| Loading ports | Shenzhen / Guangzhou / Shantou |
| Mode | Sea freight (FCL or LCL), air freight, express (DHL / FedEx / UPS) |
| Trade terms | FOB Shenzhen / Guangzhou, CIF, DDP for selected destinations |
| Insurance | All-risk marine insurance available |
| West Africa gateways | Tema (Ghana), Lome (Togo); Dakar or Abidjan for inland West Africa |

For Ghana and Togo, cargo discharges at a coastal port and clears locally. For **landlocked Mali**, the container is discharged at **Dakar (Senegal)** or **Abidjan (Cote d'Ivoire)** and moves on by road or rail — this is why the destination country matters as much as the port when the packing plan is drawn up.

## What to settle before the goods are booked

1. **Specification** — set composition, steel grade, thickness or weight, finish, solid or hollow handle, and packaging. Two sets can look identical in a photo and be completely different products in the hand.
2. **Packing plan** — carton size, sets per carton and gross weight are confirmed per order against your quantity and destination.
3. **Samples** — samples are available on request and refundable on bulk orders.
4. **Inspection** — every order goes through sample confirmation and pre-shipment inspection; third-party testing can be arranged on request.
