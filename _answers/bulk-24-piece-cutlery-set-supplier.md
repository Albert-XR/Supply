## What a standard 24-piece set contains

| Piece | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Dinner knife | 6 |
| Dinner fork | 6 |
| Dinner spoon | 6 |
| Teaspoon | 6 |

A 24-piece set is six place settings of four pieces. Some buyers prefer a five-piece place setting, or a 16-piece or 48-piece configuration for a different retail price point — the set composition is part of the specification you send us, not something fixed by the factory.

## How to start an enquiry

1. **Send the specification** — set composition, steel grade, thickness or weight, handle type, finish, and packaging style. A photo of a product you already sell works too.
2. **Confirm samples** — samples are available on request and refundable on bulk orders, so you can check weight, balance and finish in hand before committing.
3. **Agree the packing plan** — carton size, sets per carton and gross weight are confirmed per order against your quantity and destination.

There is no need to guess the order quantity up front. Tell us the destination market and what you currently sell, and we will advise on the configuration that fits it.
