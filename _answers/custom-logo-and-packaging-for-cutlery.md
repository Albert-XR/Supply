## What can actually be customised

| Element | Options |
|---|---|
| Logo | Laser engraving on the handle, the spoon bowl or the knife blade |
| Finish | Mirror polish, satin, or PVD colour such as gold, black or rainbow |
| Handle | Existing catalogue shape, or a new shape developed from your drawing |
| Packaging | Poly bag, printed colour box, magnetic gift box, display stand |
| Printed matter | Insert card, hang tag, barcode label, outer carton mark |

## The three models, briefly

| Model | Who designs it | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **OEM** | You, or an existing catalogue item | Fastest route to a branded range |
| **ODM** | The factory | A proven design you brand, sometimes with exclusivity |
| **Custom / bespoke** | Jointly, from your drawing | Strong differentiation, requires new tooling |

For a first order, the practical route is **OEM**: take a proven shape, apply your logo and packaging, test the market, then invest in bespoke tooling once you know what sells.

## How the order runs

1. **Brief** — send the set composition, finish, packaging style and your logo or reference.
2. **Proof** — we prepare the artwork placement and send it for approval.
3. **Sample confirmation** — a physical sample is made so you can approve weight, balance, engraving depth and print colour before production.
4. **Production and inspection** — every order goes through sample confirmation and pre-shipment inspection; factory audits and live video inspections are welcome.

One thing worth confirming in writing: **whether the design is exclusive to you in your market**, or whether the factory also sells the same shape to other buyers. Both arrangements are commercially normal — you just need to know which one you are buying.
